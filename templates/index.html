What is the Major System?

The Major System is a mnemonic technique that converts numbers into words by mapping each digit to a consonant sound.

Vowels and the sounds W, H, Y are ignored — they are free to use anywhere. Only consonant sounds matter.

By turning abstract numbers into concrete images, you can remember long sequences effortlessly.

The Mapping

DigitSoundsMnemonic
0S, Zzero
1T, D, Thone downstroke
2Ntwo downstrokes
3Mthree downstrokes
4Rr in four
5LL = Roman 50
6Sh, Ch, J, ZhJ mirrored = 6
7K, G (hard), NgK has two 7s
8F, Vf in script = 8
9P, BP mirrored = 9

How Encoding Works

To encode a number, find a word whose consonant sounds match the digits in order.

Notice how vowels are ignored — only the consonant sounds determine the digits.
